Section 2: Precision assurance

Precision assurance is the predictive probability that a study's posterior interval is no wider than a target width. This animation builds it for a binomial study with a Beta(2.5, 11) prior, 90% equal-tailed intervals and a target width of 0.29, and shows that it can fall as the sample size grows. At a required level of 0.90 the first-crossing rule selects 11 observations, while the sustained rule, which requires the level at every larger size, selects 13.
